About this imagery

Source: NOAA Global Mosaic of Geostationary Satellite Imagery (GMGSI), read from the NOAA Open Data bucket on AWS. Each hourly mosaic blends the nearest scans from several geostationary satellites into one global image.

Why the newest frame is never "now": the frame labelled HH:00 UTC is built from scans taken around that time, and NOAA publishes it roughly 35–40 minutes later. This site checks NOAA continuously and normally shows a new frame within a few minutes of it being published, so the latest frame is usually 40–100 minutes old.

Temperature probe: values are converted from the 0–255 mosaic counts with a simple linear approximation and are indicative only.
